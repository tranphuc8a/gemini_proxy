## A34 · Miêu tả công viên / chuyện đáng nhớ khi đi công viên
- en: Tell me about a park you often go to. Also, tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

There's a public park not far from my apartment that I visit regularly, mainly for morning jogs.
It's a medium-sized park built around a lake with a jogging path, benches, and shaded areas under tall trees.
In the mornings, it's full of life — elderly residents doing tai chi, families with young kids.
I usually go around 6 AM for a 30 to 40-minute jog before work.
One memorable incident happened a few months ago.
I was jogging while listening to music, admiring the lake view, when I tripped on an uneven patch of pavement and fell.
Knee scraped, earphones flying — pretty embarrassing, especially since people nearby saw it happen.
Fortunately, a kind woman nearby came over and offered me a wet wipe and asked if I was okay.
That small act of kindness really made me feel better.
Since then, I definitely pay more attention to where I'm running.

## A35 · Công viên: đi hồi bé với ai, làm gì, so sánh 2 công viên, việc bảo tồn
- en: Who did you go to the park with as a child, and what did you do there? Compare that park with the park you go to now. What do you think about preserving parks?
- dang: so-sanh

When I was young, my parents used to take me to a big public park near our home every Sunday morning — a real family ritual.
My brother and I would run around, kick a ball, and feed the ducks by the pond while my parents walked and chatted.
Happy memories.
Compared to the park I use now in Hanoi, the one from my childhood was much larger and felt more natural — more trees, more open grass, less urban.
My current park is well-maintained but more of an urban recreational space.
As for conservation — I think it's extremely important, especially in fast-growing cities like Hanoi.
Parks provide essential green space, cleaner air, and real mental health benefits.
I genuinely think the city should invest more in expanding and maintaining them, rather than converting green areas into commercial developments.

## B007 · Công viên bạn hay đến trông thế nào?
- en: You indicated in the survey that you go to parks with adults. Tell me about the kinds of parks that you like to visit? What do the parks look like?
- dang: mieu-ta

There are a lot of parks around where I live, but there is one I like the most.
It's a large park with many big trees.
There is also a lake with a walking path around it.
I can feel the fresh air and the open space when I am there.
The park has a lot of sports facilities such as exercise bikes and pull-up bars.
Additionally, there are also many children playing around the park, with attractions like electric trains, slides and a bubble house.

## B008 · Một lần đi công viên điển hình của bạn: làm gì, thấy gì?
- en: Describe what a typical visit to a park is like for you. Tell me about the things you do and see at parks?
- dang: thoi-quen

I usually go to the park to get some fresh air and do exercise.
Sometimes I go running, and sometimes I just take a walk alone or with a friend.
When I'm tired after a long day at work, I will go to the park and listen to music while running.
I see that most people go to the park for the same reason.
They do exercise and enjoy the fresh air.

## B009 · Lần gần nhất đi công viên: ở đâu, khi nào, làm gì từ lúc đến tới lúc về?
- en: Tell me about the last time you went to a park? Which park was it? When was it that you went? Tell me everything you did from the moment you arrived at the park to time you left.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

Last Sunday, my friends and I went to the park near my house.
We like going to the park a lot.
When we arrived, we walked around the lake and then played on the slide until we got tired.
After taking a rest and drinking some water, we played another favorite game: hide and seek.
When we became hungry and thirsty, we left the park and went to a nearby restaurant.
We ordered some food and drinks for the meal.
After that, we went home late and fell asleep right away after having a nice day.

## B009b · Kể một lần đi công viên có chuyện bất ngờ (gặp mưa).
- en: Tell me about the last time you went to a park? Which park was it? When was it that you went? Tell me everything you did from the moment you arrived at the park to time you left.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember going to a park with my family last month.
We had a small picnic and played some games together.
The weather was nice, and we enjoyed the fresh air and green trees.
However, something bad happened — it suddenly started to rain heavily.
We didn't bring any raincoats, so we all got wet and had to run to the car.
Even though it was a bit uncomfortable, we laughed a lot and it became a fun memory.

## B010 · Vì sao bạn bắt đầu đi công viên? Bây giờ đi vì lý do gì?
- en: How did you first start going to parks? What made you visit parks in the first place? Why do you go to parks now?
- dang: so-sanh

My purpose when I first went to the park was to get some fresh air.
At that time, I just enjoyed the trees and flowers there.
I usually sat on a bench and enjoyed the breeze.
But these days, I go to the park more frequently.
I go to the parks to get some exercise.
I can take a walk, ride a bicycle, or just do some simple exercise.
When I get tired after doing exercise, I can relax and enjoy the fresh air and open space of the park.
Those are really great moments for me.
