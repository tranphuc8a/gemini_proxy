## A55 · Làm gì vào ngày nghỉ?
- en: What do you usually do on your days off?
- dang: thoi-quen

On my days off, I try to balance rest, personal development, and seeing people.
I usually wake up a bit later than usual — around 7:30 — and start with a morning jog at the nearby park.
After that and a good breakfast, I might work on personal coding projects or catch up on tech articles.
In the afternoon, I'll often meet friends for lunch or coffee, or go explore a new part of the city.
I really enjoy discovering new cafes and restaurants — Hanoi has a fantastic food scene that I feel I've barely scratched the surface of.
In the evenings, I usually wind down with a movie or a few episodes of something.
Sometimes on weekends I'll take a short day trip to the countryside or a lake outside the city.
A good day off leaves me feeling genuinely recharged for the week ahead.

## A56 · Kỷ niệm về ngày nghỉ đặc biệt
- en: Tell me about a special day off that you remember. What happened?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

One day off that stands out was last year on my birthday.
My friends organized a surprise picnic at a lakeside park outside Hanoi — without telling me anything in advance.
I thought we were just going for a casual drive.
When we arrived, there were blankets, food, a cake, and a little Bluetooth speaker already set up.
We spent the whole afternoon eating, listening to music, playing games, and watching the sunset over the lake.
It was such a simple setting, but the laughter and warmth of being surrounded by people who genuinely care... it felt extraordinary.
No fancy restaurant or expensive gift could have matched that feeling.
The best memories really do come from genuine effort and thoughtfulness, not grand gestures.

## B022 · Nghỉ ở nhà, bạn muốn gặp ai và dành thời gian với ai?
- en: You indicated that you take vacations at home. Who are the people you would like to see and spend time with on your vacation?
- dang: thoi-quen

I am always busy with work so when I am on my vacation I just want to spend time with my family.
We do many things together.
We often go camping at the park nearby, it has a campsite with a great view.
We sometimes cook meals and watch some comedies.
Moreover, we also go to a restaurant nearby, we talk about our story while enjoying the food.
I feel very relaxed and happy with my family.
So, in the future, I still want to spend time with my family on a vacation.

## B023 · Kỳ nghỉ ở nhà gần nhất: làm gì từ ngày đầu đến ngày cuối, gặp ai?
- en: Describe exactly what you did during the last vacation that you spent at home? Give me a description of what you did from the first to the last day. Talk about all the people you saw and everything that you did?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I had a three-day vacation last month.
I spent the first day cleaning my house: I made the bed, cleaned the floor, washed the clothes and blankets, and also cleaned the kitchen and bathroom.
On the second day, I met some old friends at a coffee shop.
We talked about our jobs, our families and children, and discussed our plans for the future.
On the next day, I spent time hanging out with my family.
We went to the zoo and played many games.
After that, we went to our favourite restaurant.
We ate so much because we were very hungry at that time.
It was a very nice vacation I got last month.

## B024 · Kể một chuyện bất ngờ xảy ra trong kỳ nghỉ ở nhà.
- en: Could you tell me about an unusual or unexpected experience you had during a vacation you had at home? What happened? Who was involved? And why was this expericence so memorable?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I had an unusual experience last year, during a two-day vacation at home at the peak of a hot summer.
Unfortunately, the electricity in my house suddenly went out.
It was so awful for me without electricity in that hot weather: no fan, no air conditioner, no cooking, no television.
I called the electric company to fix the problem, but it took two days of repairs.
It took my whole vacation.
That was a really terrible vacation.

## B075 · Người nước bạn tụ họp, ăn mừng thế nào?
- en: Talk about gatherings or celebrations in your country? What do people do when they get together to celebrate things?
- dang: mieu-ta

In my country, people often go to restaurants for gatherings or celebrations.
They enjoy food and drinks with their friends.
During the celebration, they eat, drink, cheer together, and often have wine or beer.
They talk loudly and sometimes even sing together.
They leave all their work behind and enjoy this moment.
It makes their relationship closer and more intimate.

## B076 · Lần tụ họp gần nhất của bạn: dịp gì, làm gì?
- en: What did you do at your last gathering or celebration? What was the occasion? Give me all the details?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

A few weeks ago, I had a team-building event with my team members in my family's garden.
Some members went fishing, and some chased and caught chickens to prepare for lunch.
Since all of us are office workers, we were not good at cooking, so it was really hard for us to make a meal by ourselves.
However, we were good at teamwork and knew how to support each other well.
In the end, we had a delicious meal, and it turned into a meaningful day of gathering.
It was such a great occasion for our team.

## B077 · Kể một sự cố đáng nhớ trong một buổi tụ họp.
- en: Talk about a memorable incident that happened at a gathering or a celebration? Why was it memorable? What made it unforgettable?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember going to my friend's house for his last birthday party.
We had dinner together and had some drinks.
However, I drank too much and got drunk.
My stomach was upset and I had a light headache.
I felt like I was hanging from a tree, and I could not walk normally.
I still had a hangover the next day.
It took me quite a long time to sober up.
Since then, I've told myself to be more careful when I'm drinking.

## B093 · Người nước bạn đi đâu khi rảnh? Biển, công viên hay nơi nào khác?
- en: Where do people in your country go to in their free time? Do they go to beaches? Do they go to parks or any other places? What are some popular locations that people go in their free time?
- dang: y-kien

In my country, people often gather or travel when they have free time.
When they gather, they usually go to a bar or a restaurant to eat and drink with their friends.
When they travel, they often go with family or friends to beautiful places like mountains, beaches, or parks.
Of course, some people just stay at home and take a rest in their free time.
So these are the common things people do in my country when they are free.

## B094 · Thời gian rảnh của bạn ngày xưa nhiều hay ít hơn bây giờ? Khác thế nào?
- en: Talk about your free time in the past. Did you have more free time or less free time back then? How was it different from your free time now?
- dang: so-sanh

I had much more free time in the past when I was a student.
At that time, I only went to school for half a day, so I had the rest of the day free.
I usually hung out with my friends or played online games.
Sometimes, I also studied by myself.
But now, after I graduated, got a job, especially when I got married with two children, I'm very busy.
I rarely have free time.
Usually, I only have a little free time at the end of the day when my kids are asleep.
At that time, I just use my phone to read some news or online articles before going to bed.
So my free time now is very different from when I was a student.

## B095 · Lần gần nhất bạn có thời gian rảnh: khi nào, làm gì, với ai?
- en: Tell me about the last time you had some free time When was it? What did you do? Who did you spend time with?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

The last time I had free time was two weeks ago when I took my company's annual leave.
On the first day, I just stayed at home.
I listened to music, watched some movies, and enjoyed my meals.
On the next day, I took my child to the park, the zoo, and a children's play center.
The weather was so nice that my child enjoyed outdoor activities.
We felt very relaxed at that time.
Hopefully, I will have more free time in the future.

## B096 · Những ngày lễ phổ biến ở nước bạn: mọi người ăn mừng ở đâu, làm gì?
- en: Tell me about some popular holidays in your country. Where do people typically celebrate these holidays? What kinds of thing do they do to celebrate?
- dang: mieu-ta

There are three big holidays in Vietnam.
They are Lunar New Year's Day, Victory Day, and National Day.
On these days, people have time off from work and they gather together.
They visit their families in their hometowns, meet friends, neighbors, and cook special meals.
They also exchange lots of presents.
These holidays are important break times in the working year for people in my country.

## B097 · Một kỷ niệm ngày lễ hồi nhỏ: ở đâu, nhớ gì?
- en: Tell me about a holiday memory from your childhood. Tell me where you were and what that place looked like. Tell me everything that you remember from that holiday.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember a memory from Lunar New Year's Day when I was a kid.
Like other children, I liked playing with fireworks during the holiday.
But one day, I had an accident — the firework exploded and burned my hand.
It was really painful, and I had to go to the hospital to have the wound treated and bandaged.
Since that time, I never play with fireworks anymore.
It was a really bad experience.
