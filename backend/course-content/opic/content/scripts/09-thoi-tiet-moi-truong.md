## A40 · Thời tiết ở thành phố của bạn – so sánh với thành phố khác và quá khứ
- en: What is the weather like in your city? How is it different from other cities, and from the past?
- dang: so-sanh

Hanoi has a four-season climate, which makes it quite different from Ho Chi Minh City in the south.
Ho Chi Minh City basically has two seasons — dry and rainy — and stays warm all year, around 25 to 35 degrees.
Hanoi, on the other hand, has proper winters that can drop to around 10 degrees, mild springs, hot summers, and cool autumns.
Personally, I love Hanoi's spring — the weather is mild, breezy, and really comfortable for outdoor activities.
Compared to the past, though, I feel the weather has become more extreme in recent years.
Summers seem longer and hotter, winters less predictable.
I think part of it is the urban heat island effect as the city expands, and part of it is broader climate change.
It's a concerning trend.

## A41 · Thời tiết ở nước bạn – so sánh với nước khác
- en: Tell me about the weather in your country. How is it different from the weather in other countries?
- dang: so-sanh

Vietnam has a tropical monsoon climate with significant regional variation.
The south has two seasons — dry from November to April, rainy from May to October.
The north, where I live, has four proper seasons.
Compared to countries like South Korea or Japan, Vietnam is significantly warmer on average.
When I visited Seoul for work, the winter temperatures were well below zero — nothing like what I experience in Hanoi.
In terms of rainfall, Vietnam gets a lot during monsoon season — heavy downpours that can cause localized flooding, especially in Hanoi.
The heat and humidity here in summer can feel pretty intense to visitors from more temperate countries.
But I do think the lush greenery and vibrant nature that comes with a tropical climate is one of Vietnam's great natural assets.

## A42 · Sự kiện đáng nhớ xảy ra do thời tiết, cách bạn giải quyết
- en: Tell me about a memorable event caused by the weather. How did you deal with it?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

A weather event that stands out was a strong typhoon that hit Hanoi a few years ago.
The city issued warnings the night before, so I stocked up on food and water in advance.
The next morning the wind was incredibly strong and the rain relentless.
I decided to work from home.
But around midday, the power went out and stayed out for about six hours.
Without electricity I couldn't work or charge anything.
My laptop battery lasted about three hours, which I used for the most urgent tasks.
I ended up eating cold food and spending the afternoon reading a paperback by the window — honestly not the worst afternoon.
When the power came back, I charged everything immediately and made a note to buy a proper backup power bank.
The experience really highlighted how dependent we are on electricity in modern life.

## A43 · Thời tiết thay đổi những năm gần đây? Nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng
- en: Has the weather changed in recent years? What do you think are the causes and effects of these changes?
- dang: y-kien

I've definitely noticed that weather patterns in Vietnam have become more unpredictable and extreme in recent years.
Summers in Hanoi are getting hotter — temperatures regularly exceed 40 degrees during peak months.
Even a short rainstorm can now cause significant flooding in parts of the city.
Winters, on the other hand, have become shorter and milder, disrupting the traditional seasonal rhythm.
I think the main causes are global climate change driven by greenhouse gas emissions, combined with rapid urbanization that reduces green cover and amplifies the heat island effect.
The consequences are significant — crop failures, more severe flooding in coastal regions, and real health impacts from heat stress.
On a personal level, the increasingly hot summers are just uncomfortable — they affect how much time I can spend outdoors.
I think individuals, businesses, and governments all share responsibility to reduce emissions and adapt to these changes.

## B044 · Thời tiết nơi bạn sống: từng mùa thế nào, bạn thích mùa nào?
- en: Tell me about the weather at where you live? What is the weather like in each season? Which season do you personally like?
- dang: mieu-ta

Vietnam has four distinct seasons: spring, summer, autumn, and winter.
The weather is very nice in spring and autumn.
The temperature is cool, and it's good for outdoor activities.
On the other hand, summer is extremely hot.
Moreover, sometimes it suddenly rains heavily with dangerous thunder.
Winter is the complete opposite.
It's freezing cold and there is no rain.
Of course, I like spring and autumn the most, for the reasons I mentioned before.

## B046 · Thời tiết nước bạn thay đổi thế nào so với hồi bạn còn nhỏ?
- en: How has the weather in your country changed over the years? What was the weather like when you were a child? How is it different from now?
- dang: so-sanh

Vietnam still has four distinct seasons now.
But spring and summer have become longer, while winter and autumn have become shorter.
Also, summer seems much hotter and it has a lot of rain.
When it rains heavily, many places get flooded.
Sometimes people die, and many houses and fields are damaged by the summer rain.

## B047 · Kể một trải nghiệm thời tiết khắc nghiệt (bão, lũ...) và cách mọi người ứng phó.
- en: Severe weather conditions can do a lot of damage. Tell me about an experience you had related to severe weather conditions. Perhaps a city was flooded or maybe businesses or schools were closed due to heavy rain, what was the problem. How did people deal with the situation?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember when a strong typhoon hit Vietnam last summer.
There were strong winds and heavy rain, and floods were everywhere.
Many people lost their lives, and many houses, cars, and fields were underwater.
It was a terrible situation, and it took a long time to recover from the damage.

## B059 · Bạn tái chế những loại đồ gì?
- en: Recycling is a common practice in many places. Tell me about all the different kinds of items that you recycle
- dang: thoi-quen

I gather recyclables at home every day.
I put cans, bottles, plastic, paper, and glass into the recycling bin.
On average, I take them out once a week, usually on my way to work.
I think recycling is a good habit because it helps us reuse resources and protect the environment.

## B060 · Hồi nhỏ việc tái chế diễn ra thế nào? Bạn mang đồ tái chế đến đâu?
- en: Tell me what recycling was like when you were a child. Was there a particular place to which you took out the recyclables? Describe what it was like and what you did in detail?
- dang: so-sanh

When I was a child, I wasn't aware of the benefits of recycling, so I didn't usually do it.
I only gathered paper at school sometimes because my teacher asked me to.
But now I know recycling is a good habit, and I try to do it everywhere and whenever I can.
I recycle everything possible — like cans, bottles, plastic, paper, glass, and even metals.
