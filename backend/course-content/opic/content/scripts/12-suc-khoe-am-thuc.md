## B064 · Những loại thực phẩm nào tốt cho sức khoẻ, và vì sao?
- en: Many people try to eat healthy these days. What kinds of food are healthy and why are they healthy for us?
- dang: y-kien

Nowadays, people understand food and health better than in the past, so they try to eat healthier.
First, they eat vegetables and fruits as often as they can, because these contain a lot of vitamins and fiber.
Next, they eat fish, meat, and beans, which are rich in protein.
Also, many people choose organic food whenever possible, since it is not grown with chemicals and so it is much healthier.
So, these are the types of food that are considered healthy.

## B065 · Bạn biết đến ăn uống lành mạnh thế nào? Hồi nhỏ gia đình bạn ăn uống ra sao?
- en: Tell me how you found out about eating healthy. Did your family eat healthy when you were growing up? Describe to me in detail how you started to eat healthy?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

When I was a kid, my parents really cared about food and health.
They made me eat vegetables and fruits every day, and they also made me drink milk.
But when I grew older, I started choosing my own food.
Because I'm very busy on weekdays, I often eat fast food, even though I know it's not good for my health.
I think I should take care more about food that I eat to protect my health.

## B066 · Món ăn phổ biến ở nước bạn là gì? Có gì đặc biệt?
- en: What is a popular dish in your country? What is special about the dish? Please describle that dish in detail?
- dang: mieu-ta

The most popular dish in Vietnam is Pho.
You can find it everywhere, from the North to the South, from inland to the islands.
Moreover, some Vietnamese people bring Pho when they go abroad, so now it is also well known in many countries around the world.
Pho is a type of noodle soup, usually made with beef broth and spices, topped with rice noodles and thinly sliced beef or chicken.
Some vegetables and onions are also added.
It has a very unique taste that you cannot find in any other dish.
When I travel, I always try Pho at that place to compare and find out where the best pho is.
And actually, I often eat Pho for breakfast.

## B067 · Mô tả một người khoẻ mạnh bạn biết. Điều gì khiến họ khoẻ?
- en: Describe a healthy person you know? What makes that person healthy? Tell me everything about the thing that make that person healthier?
- dang: mieu-ta

My wife is a very healthy person.
She always makes time to work out or exercise whenever she can.
She goes to the gym twice a week, she usually goes swimming on weekends, and walks for about 30 minutes every morning.
Plus, she always eats healthy.
She says no to fast food or junk food.
She has a strong and beautiful body.
I really admire her and I try to follow her habits to become healthier.

## B068 · Bạn từng thay đổi thói quen nào vì sức khoẻ chưa? Kể về sự thay đổi đó.
- en: Have you ever changed a habit or a certain lifestyle for your health? Maybe you started to work out or started to eat healthy. Tell me about that change you made?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

In the past, I was really lazy.
I ate too much and didn't exercise.
My body became very big and I was overweight.
I often got sick and had to go to the hospital more frequently.
At that point, I decided to change myself.
I cut down on the food I ate and did hard exercise every day.
Day by day, I felt the changes in my body.
I felt better inside, I lost some weight, and I got sick less often.
I will continue doing this to stay strong and healthy.

## B069 · Kể chi tiết một việc bạn làm vì sức khoẻ và tác động của nó.
- en: Talk about one thing that you did for your health in detail. What kind of impact did it have on your health? Give me all the details about the effect that had on your health
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I am a very busy person, so I don't have much time to take care of my health.
But I always spend 15 minutes every morning to do push-ups.
It's a short amount of exercise, but I think it has a big impact on my health.
After doing push-ups, I feel strong and full of energy for a long working day.
My body also gets a nice shape when I keep doing push-ups every day.
Push-ups are simple, but they have a big effect on my health.

## B070 · Nhà hàng ở nước bạn trông thế nào? Thường bán món gì?
- en: I would like to know about restaurants in your country. What do typical restaurants look like? What kinds of food do they commonly offer?
- dang: mieu-ta

There are many kinds of restaurants in my country, such as fast food restaurants, traditional restaurants, and BBQ restaurants.
But these days, BBQ restaurants are the most popular and trending.
I can find them easily almost everywhere.
They serve many kinds of meat such as pork, beef, chicken, duck, and lamb.
There are also many types of seafood like shrimp, crab, fish, octopus, and squid.
It is so great to eat grilled meat with sauce.
Among them, seafood BBQ usually attracts the largest number of diners.

## B071 · Lần gần nhất đi ăn nhà hàng: loại gì, thực đơn, ăn gì, đi với ai?
- en: Tell me about a restaurant you ate out recently. What kind of restaurant was it? What was their menu and what did you eat? Who did you go with? Did you like how the food tasted?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember going to a seafood BBQ restaurant with my friend last week.
It's a well-known restaurant in my area.
The food is delicious, the price is cheap, and the service is also good.
There were many kinds of seafood, such as salmon, shrimp, crab, squid, and octopus.
We had to wait a long time because the restaurant was very crowded.
The meal was so tasty.
We enjoyed the food with some beer.
We were satisfied with the meal.
It was a great dinner.

## B072 · Nhà hàng bạn hay đến hồi nhỏ: trông thế nào, ăn gì, nhớ gì nhất?
- en: Now, tell me about a restaurant you used to go to as a child. What was it like? What did you eat? Who did you go with? What do you remember most about that place? Tell me about that restaurant in as much detail as possible.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember there was a fast food restaurant near my house when I was a kid.
It was a small restaurant, but it always had a lot of diners.
I used to go there on weekends with my parents.
They served many types of dishes, such as pizza, hamburgers, and Korean noodles.
I usually ordered my favorite seafood pizza and a bottle of Coca-Cola.
The pizza tasted amazing.
But now, that restaurant has moved to another place, and I don't know where it is.
I really miss it and the delicious food of that restaurant.

## B073 · Người bận rộn thường ăn uống thế nào vào ngày thường?
- en: How do busy working people usually get their meals on weekdays? Do they order food or go to restaurants? What do they usually do?
- dang: y-kien

People have become a lot busier than in the past.
Sometimes, they don't even have time to cook for themselves, so they eat out or order in much more often.
At the same time, people also care more about their health than before.
Even when they eat out, they think about eating healthy.
So, they tend to choose restaurants that offer healthy menus.
This has become a trend: people eat out more often, but they focus on healthier options.
