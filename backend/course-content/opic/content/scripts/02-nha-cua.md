## A08 · Miêu tả nhà của bạn / miêu tả chi tiết ngôi nhà đang sống
- en: I would like to know where you live. Can you describe your home to me in detail? What does it look like?
- dang: mieu-ta

So right now, I'm living in a one-bedroom apartment on the 8th floor of a residential building in Cau Giay District, Hanoi.
I live by myself, which was a conscious choice — I like having a quiet, focused space after a full day of coding and meetings.
The apartment has a living room that opens into a small kitchen, one bedroom, and a bathroom.
It's compact, but everything's there.
In the living room, there's a sofa, a TV, and a small bookshelf.
The kitchen is pretty basic — I mainly use it for coffee and simple meals.
My bedroom is probably my favorite part.
It has a comfortable bed, a desk by the window, and a wardrobe.
The location is also really convenient — close to my office, supermarkets, and a nearby park.
Overall, I'm quite happy with it.

## A10 · So sánh nhà ở quá khứ và hiện tại
- en: Compare the home you live in now with the home you lived in in the past. How are they different?
- dang: so-sanh

My current place is really quite different from the house I grew up in.
When I was young, we lived in a two-story house in a smaller city — spacious, with a front yard, a big kitchen, and everyone had their own room.
The surroundings were peaceful — trees, neighbors who knew each other, fresh air in the mornings.
It was lovely.
Now I'm in a small one-bedroom apartment in the city with no outdoor space, which I do miss sometimes.
But the location is hard to beat — I'm close to everything I need: work, restaurants, cafes, a park.
The city noise can get a bit much at times, but overall, I appreciate the convenience.
Both places have their charm, and I carry good memories from my childhood home.

## A11 · Miêu tả nhà, phòng thích nhất và sự thay đổi đồ đạc trong nhà
- en: Describe your home and your favorite room. Have you changed any furniture or items in your home recently?
- dang: mieu-ta

My apartment's layout is simple — living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom.
The bedroom is definitely my sanctuary.
When I first moved in, the apartment came with very basic furnishings — pretty bare, honestly.
Over the past two years I've gradually made it feel more like home.
The biggest purchase was probably a new ergonomic desk chair, because I spend a lot of hours working from home and my back was suffering.
I also got a small bookshelf and some indoor plants to make the living room feel warmer.
Most recently I replaced my old mattress with a better quality one, and the difference in sleep quality has been noticeable.
I like the idea of slowly improving the space over time — it doesn't need to be perfect all at once.

## A12 · Nếu mua đồ nội thất mới: mua ở đâu, cách tìm như thế nào?
- en: If you needed to buy new furniture, where would you go, and how would you look for it?
- dang: y-kien

If I needed to buy new furniture, I'd always start by researching online first before going to any store.
I usually check platforms like Tiki or Lazada, compare prices, and read through customer reviews.
For smaller things like shelves or lamps, buying online is fine as long as the reviews are solid.
For bigger items — a sofa, a bed frame — I prefer going to a physical store to see the quality and size for myself.
There's a furniture store near my area I've visited a couple of times and found pretty good.
I also browse Instagram and Pinterest for design inspiration before I decide on anything.
My main priorities are always durability, functionality, and whether it fits both the room and my budget.
Good furniture is worth investing in — it directly affects how comfortable your everyday life is.

## A13 · Nếu thiết kế lại phòng: cách thức và lý do
- en: If you could redesign your room, what would you change, and why?
- dang: y-kien

If I could redesign my bedroom, I'd start by repainting the walls — probably a soft warm tone like light beige or sage green, instead of the plain white they are now.
I think color really does affect mood, and something warmer would make the room feel a lot cozier.
I'd also rearrange the furniture a bit.
Right now my desk placement is a bit awkward — I'd move it to get better natural light.
I'd add some wall shelving to store books and decorative items without taking up floor space.
And better blackout curtains, definitely — the current ones let in way too much light in the morning.
The main goal is to create a space that's both restful and inspiring — somewhere I can properly relax, but also focus when I need to.

## A15 · Vấn đề tại nơi bạn sống / cách giải quyết
- en: Tell me about a problem you have had at the place where you live. What was it, and how did you solve it?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

Living in an apartment building comes with a few recurring issues.
The most persistent one is noise — the building's on a fairly busy street, so I can hear traffic, especially at night.
I started using earplugs when I sleep, and honestly it's helped a lot.
Another issue was when the water heater broke down last winter.
I had to take cold showers for about three days, which was pretty miserable.
I reported it to building management and they got a technician in within two days, so that was resolved fairly quickly.
There was also a week when the elevator was out of service — so eight flights of stairs, every single day.
That one was painful.
For these kinds of problems, I've found the best approach is just to report them to management promptly and follow up if they're taking too long.

## A16 · Vấn đề nơi bạn sống: họp gia đình bàn bạc, cách giải quyết
- en: Tell me about a problem at your home that your family discussed together. How did you solve it?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

When I still lived with my family, we'd occasionally have to sit down together to discuss household problems.
One of the more serious ones was when the roof started leaking during the rainy season — it was affecting multiple rooms and the humidity was damaging some furniture.
My dad wanted to fix it immediately; my mum was more concerned about the cost.
That kind of disagreement is pretty natural.
After talking it through at dinner, we agreed to hire a professional contractor to do a proper repair rather than a quick patch-up.
It took about two weeks, but the result was much better than we expected.
That experience really showed me how important it is to tackle household problems quickly — and as a team.

## A66 · Đồ gia dụng gặp vấn đề và cách xử lý
- en: Tell me about a time when an appliance in your home broke down. How did you deal with it?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

The most disruptive appliance issue I've had was when my refrigerator stopped cooling properly.
I noticed one morning when my food was no longer cold and the milk had gone off overnight.
I checked the basics first — making sure it was plugged in properly, checking the thermostat settings.
That didn't help.
So I looked up the brand's customer service number online and called to describe the problem.
They scheduled a technician for the following afternoon.
The technician found a faulty compressor and said it would take three to four days to repair.
During that time I stored perishables in a cooler with ice and kept grocery shopping to daily essentials.
After the repair, the fridge worked perfectly.
And importantly, it was still under warranty — so the repair was covered.
That reminded me to always check warranty status before agreeing to pay for repairs.
