## A27 · Tả chuyến du lịch trong nước
- en: Tell me about a trip you took in your country. Where did you go, and what did you do there?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

One of my most memorable domestic trips was to Da Nang with three close friends about two years ago.
We stayed for four days and three nights, which felt like just the right amount.
We checked out the Marble Mountains, My Khe Beach, the Dragon Bridge, and Ba Na Hills.
The highlight for me was spending a full day at the beach — swimming in the morning and just relaxing under the sun in the afternoon.
The seafood was outstanding.
We found this small local restaurant near the beach with incredibly fresh grilled fish and shrimp at really affordable prices.
One funny memory was getting lost on the way there and stumbling upon a lively local night market by accident.
We ended up spending an hour browsing — one of those unplanned moments that turns out to be the best part of the trip.
I'd love to go back someday.

## A28 · Miêu tả điểm du lịch (ở đâu, làm gì, với ai)
- en: Describe a place you have traveled to. Where is it, what did you do there, and who did you go with?
- dang: mieu-ta

I'd like to describe a trip to Hoi An — a charming ancient town in central Vietnam.
I went with two university friends and we spent three days exploring.
Hoi An is famous for its well-preserved old quarter, lantern-lit streets, and traditional crafts.
During the day we wandered through the old town, had some custom clothes made at a tailor, and rented bicycles to explore the rice paddies and nearby beaches.
At night, the town transforms into something magical — streets glowing with colorful lanterns reflected in the river.
We had dinner at a traditional restaurant overlooking the water and I still remember how peaceful it was.
What made the trip special wasn't just the scenery but the pace.
Hoi An felt like stepping back in time, and it reminded me how valuable it is to slow down sometimes.

## A29 · Miêu tả một quốc gia bạn đã đi nghỉ ở nước ngoài
- en: Tell me about a country you have visited on vacation. What was it like?
- dang: mieu-ta

I had the chance to visit South Korea for a short work trip, and I made the most of my days off to do some sightseeing.
I visited Seoul, and I was immediately struck by how clean, organized, and efficient the city is.
The metro system was incredibly convenient — easy to navigate even without knowing much Korean.
I visited Gyeongbokgung Palace, which was stunning — traditional Korean architecture surrounded by modern skyscrapers.
I also explored Insadong for traditional crafts and Myeongdong for street food and shopping.
The food was a real highlight — bibimbap, Korean BBQ, tteokbokki... all delicious.
What impressed me most was how ancient culture and cutting-edge technology coexist so seamlessly.
It was a short visit, but it left a strong impression.
I'd love to go back for a proper holiday.

## A30 · Lý do mọi người thích đi du lịch? Lý do nào quan trọng nhất?
- en: Why do you think people like to travel? Which reason do you think is the most important?
- dang: y-kien

I think people travel for all sorts of reasons, and each one is completely valid.
Some people travel to relax and escape the stress of daily life — just getting away from familiar surroundings can be incredibly refreshing.
Others go to experience new cultures, food, and perspectives.
And many people travel for adventure — hiking, diving, exploring landscapes they've never seen.
And of course, some travel specifically to spend quality time with family or friends and create shared memories.
If I had to pick the most important reason... I'd say gaining new perspectives.
Travel forces you out of your comfort zone and shows you there are many different, equally valid ways of living.
That kind of experience makes you more empathetic, open-minded, and genuinely grateful for your own life.

## A31 · Kỷ niệm về chuyến du lịch đáng nhớ
- en: Tell me about a memorable trip you have taken. What happened that made it so memorable?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

The most memorable travel experience I've had was a solo trip to Sapa in northern Vietnam.
It was my first time traveling completely alone, which felt both exciting and a little daunting, to be honest.
I took an overnight train from Hanoi and arrived in the early morning surrounded by misty mountains and rice terraces.
It was breathtaking.
I joined a local trekking group and spent two days hiking through villages of ethnic minority communities.
One moment that really stood out — a local family invited me into their home for tea.
We couldn't really communicate because of the language barrier, but the warmth and hospitality they showed me was genuinely touching.
That experience taught me that human connection doesn't always need words — kindness is universal.
It remains one of the most meaningful trips I've ever taken.

## A32 · Những thứ cần chuẩn bị khi đi du lịch
- en: What do you do to prepare for a trip? Tell me about everything you need to get ready.
- dang: thoi-quen

I like to plan carefully, but without over-scheduling everything.
The first thing I do is research the destination — must-see places, local food, weather, transport options.
Then I book accommodation and transport in advance, especially if it's peak season.
For packing, I have a checklist: weather-appropriate clothes, toiletries, medication, a basic first-aid kit, and copies of important documents.
I always bring a portable power bank since I rely heavily on my phone for navigation, translation, and photos.
I also download offline maps and save key addresses in case I lose internet connection.
A bit of local cash is always useful, and I make sure to share my itinerary with a family member so someone knows where I am.
Good preparation really does let you relax and enjoy the trip.

## A33 · Miêu tả nơi muốn du lịch và đưa lời khuyên cho Jiwon
- en: Describe a place you would like to visit. Also, give some travel advice to Jiwon, who is planning to visit your country.
- dang: y-kien

A place I've always wanted to visit is Japan — specifically Kyoto during cherry blossom season.
I'm drawn to Japan because of its perfect blend of ancient tradition and modern innovation.
Kyoto appeals to me for its temples, tea houses, bamboo groves, and peaceful gardens.
I imagine walking through the Arashiyama bamboo forest in the early morning, before the crowds arrive, would be extraordinary.
If I were advising my Korean colleague Jiwon on visiting Vietnam, I'd say: start with Hoi An.
It's beautiful, walkable, and gives you a completely different side of Vietnam from the busy cities.
Go early in the morning to explore the old town before the heat kicks in.
And don't miss Cao Lau — a local noodle dish that's only made properly in Hoi An.
Absolutely delicious.
Best time to visit is February to April, when the weather is dry and pleasant.

## B012 · Những nơi bạn thích đi du lịch trong nước và lý do?

- dang: mieu-ta

There are a lot of beautiful places in Vietnam.
But there's one I like the most: Ha Long Bay.
It is a bay in the north of Vietnam.
I love it there because I like walking along the shore, gazing at the sea and the starry sky in the evening.
And the natural scenery there is very beautiful: there are thousands of small islands in Ha Long Bay with different shapes.
I stand on the ship, go around the islands and see their beauty clearly.
Additionally, there are many kinds of seafood, and the prices are quite affordable.
This is why I make it a point to visit Ha Long Bay every summer.

## B013 · Bạn chuẩn bị những gì trước mỗi chuyến đi?
- en: Can you tell me about the things you do in order to prepare for trips?
- dang: thoi-quen

I'm a light packer, so when I travel, I only bring the essentials.
Firstly, I pack a couple of clothes depending on the weather.
Then I add some basic emergency medicine.
I also bring a hat, sunglasses and a swimsuit.
Of course, I need a toothbrush, toothpaste, and shampoo for the trip.
And I cannot forget to bring my wallet, my phone and a charger.
That is all I need for my trip.

## B014 · Kể một trải nghiệm du lịch khó quên: khi nào, ở đâu, với ai, chuyện gì xảy ra?
- en: Tell me about one travel experience you had that was unforgettable. Start by telling me when this happened, where you were, and who you were with. Tell me about all the things that happended that make this experience so unforgettable?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

Last summer, during a company team-building trip, I had quite an unpleasant experience.
After lunch, I believe I ate something that was not fresh.
Soon after, my stomach started hurting, and I developed food poisoning.
I had a high fever and a severe headache throughout the afternoon.
I had to keep running to the bathroom due to diarrhea.
To improve my condition, I went to a pharmacy and got some medicine.
Fortunately, after taking the medicine, I started feeling better.
However, I had to stay in my room and rest the next day, missing out on all the activities during the trip.
Since then, I've learned to be much more cautious about what I eat, especially in unfamiliar situations.
