## AR01 · Chuyến du lịch có vấn đề – gọi điện cho đại lý đổi lịch
- en: I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. There is a problem with the trip you booked, so you need to change the schedule. Call the travel agency and ask to reschedule.
- dang: dien

Hi, this is Son calling.
I booked a two-day trip to Da Nang through your agency last week — it was scheduled for this Saturday the 10th.
Something unexpected has come up at work, so unfortunately I won't be able to travel on that date.
Is there any chance I could reschedule to the following Saturday, the 17th?
Could you also check whether the hotel and transport arrangements can be moved to the new date as well?
If there are any rescheduling fees, just let me know the amount and how I can take care of it.
I'd really appreciate your help.
Could you give me a call back once you've confirmed availability?
Thank you so much.

I'd really appreciate a quick resolution — thank you.

## AR09 · Điện thoại mới mua bị lỗi – yêu cầu 2 giải pháp từ đại lý

I have the receipt and original packaging here with me.

First, could you replace this unit with a brand-new one of the same model?
Alternatively, if that's not possible, could the phone be sent for warranty repair, with a temporary device provided in the meantime?
This is my primary work phone, so I'd really appreciate a quick resolution.
Thank you for your help.

## AR11 · Cửa sổ hỏng – gọi người sửa; gọi lại khi mãi không thấy tới
- en: A window in your apartment is broken. Call the building manager to report it. Then call again, because the repairman has not arrived.
- dang: tinh-huong

(Call 1 – Báo sự cố)
Hello, I'm calling to report a problem in my apartment — I'm in unit 812, Building A.
One of my bedroom windows is broken — the latch is completely stuck and I can't close it properly.
Could you arrange for a technician to come and fix it?
Rain is expected this afternoon and leaving it open could cause water damage.
Would it be possible to have someone come this morning?
(Call 2 – Follow-up)
Hello again, this is Son from unit 812.
I called this morning about the broken window and was told a technician would arrive by 2 PM.
It's now 3:30 and no one has come yet.
Could you check on the status of my request?
It's become quite urgent — it's already started raining and water is coming in through the gap.
Could you either send someone in the next 30 minutes, or at least let me know when to expect them?
I'll be available for the rest of the afternoon.
Thank you.

## AR13 · Đến tiệm bảo cắt tóc / cắt không ưng, yêu cầu cắt lại

## AR16 · Kể về chuyến thăm một gia đình người bạn nước ngoài
- en: Have you ever visited the family of a friend from another country? Tell me about that experience in detail.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I'd like to share a really memorable experience from when I visited my Korean colleague Jiwon's family home during a work trip to Seoul last year.
Jiwon very kindly invited me to have dinner at his parents' house, which I thought was a wonderful gesture.
When I arrived, his parents greeted me so warmly — even though they spoke very little English.
They'd prepared an elaborate traditional Korean dinner — galbi, japchae, all sorts of banchan side dishes, and of course kimchi.
I was a little nervous about the dining etiquette, but Jiwon guided me gently and made sure I felt comfortable the whole time.
What touched me most was the effort his family put into making a foreign guest feel genuinely welcome.
Despite the language barrier, the warmth communicated everything.
It was a humbling and heartwarming experience — a real reminder of how much we share across cultures when we approach each other with kindness.
