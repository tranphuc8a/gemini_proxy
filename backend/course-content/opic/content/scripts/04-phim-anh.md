## A22 · Bộ phim yêu thích của bạn là gì?
- en: What is your favorite movie? Tell me about it, and why you like it.
- dang: mieu-ta

My all-time favorite movie is Interstellar, directed by Christopher Nolan.
It's a sci-fi film that manages to combine really complex ideas about space, time, and physics with an incredibly emotional story — a father trying to save his daughter and humanity.
What I love most is how it's both intellectually stimulating and deeply moving at the same time.
That's not easy to pull off.
The visual effects are breathtaking — especially the scenes depicting black holes.
And the Hans Zimmer soundtrack is one of the best I've ever heard.
I've watched it three times now, and each time I notice something new.
For me, a great movie is one that stays with you long after it ends — and Interstellar definitely does that.

## A23 · Thể loại phim yêu thích / thể loại phim xem gần đây
- en: What kind of movies do you like? What movies have you watched recently?
- dang: mieu-ta

My favorite genres are sci-fi and psychological thrillers — I'm really drawn to stories that challenge the way you think.
Sci-fi lets me explore big questions about space, technology, and the future in an entertaining way.
Thrillers keep me hooked because of the tension and unpredictability.
Recently I've also been watching more Korean films and dramas, which tend to have really strong storytelling and character development.
I watched Parasite last year and was completely blown away — the layered social commentary, the plot twists... just incredible.
On weekends, I usually watch a movie or a couple of episodes of something at home.
I think good films are one of the most efficient ways to learn about different cultures and the human experience.

## A24 · Đi xem phim cùng bạn thường làm gì?
- en: What do you usually do when you go to the movies with your friends? Tell me everything from beginning to end.
- dang: thoi-quen

Going to the cinema with friends is always a great time from start to finish.
We usually plan a few days ahead — figure out what to watch and book tickets online to avoid the queue.
Before the movie, we almost always grab a meal first, usually at a nearby restaurant or food court.
At the cinema, I always get a medium popcorn and a drink — that's just a non-negotiable ritual for me.
Can't skip it.
During the film we stay quiet, but you can always feel the group's reactions — laughing together or gasping at the same moment.
That shared experience is really something.
After, we usually find a café nearby and talk about the film.
Those post-movie discussions are honestly my favorite part — everyone has different observations and interpretations.

## A25 · Sự kiện trước/sau khi xem phim tại rạp
- en: What do you usually do before and after watching a movie at the theater?
- dang: thoi-quen

A typical cinema trip for me follows a pretty enjoyable routine.
First, I research the film — read a brief synopsis and watch the trailer to decide if it's worth the big screen.
Then I book tickets through an app, which is way more convenient than queuing at the box office.
I try to arrive about 20 to 30 minutes early so there's time to get snacks without rushing.
The cinema experience itself is special — the big screen, surround sound, and the shared atmosphere with other viewers is something you really can't replicate at home.
After the movie, if I'm with friends we always dissect it over drinks or dessert somewhere nearby.
If watched alone, I'll read reviews online and compare perspectives.

## A26 · Nam diễn viên được yêu thích nhất
- en: Who is your favorite actor? Why do you like him?
- dang: mieu-ta

My favorite actor is Tom Hanks.
I've admired him for years because of his incredible range and authenticity.
What impresses me most is that he makes you believe every single role — whether it's Cast Away, Forrest Gump, or The Da Vinci Code.
He never seems to be 'performing' — it just feels completely genuine.
I also deeply respect him as a person.
From everything I've read, he's known as one of the kindest and most humble people in Hollywood.
Among Korean actors, I really admire Song Kang-ho, who starred in Parasite.
His ability to play morally complex characters is remarkable.
Good acting, to me, is about making the audience forget they're watching a film — and both of these actors achieve that effortlessly.

## B005 · Lần gần nhất đi xem phim: đi với ai, trước và sau khi xem làm gì?
- en: Tell me about when you went to the movies recently. Who did you go with and how was that day? What did you do before the movie and what did you after?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

Last weekend was my friend's birthday.
We threw a surprise party for her, then we drove to the cinema to watch an action movie.
Before the movie, we bought some popcorn and soft drinks like Coca-Cola and orange juice.
After the movie, we went to a nearby fast-food restaurant and ordered fried chicken with some snacks.
We were very hungry, and the food was so good — it was a very delicious meal.
Moreover, after the meal, we each got an ice cream.
We were totally full after all that.
It was a nice and memorable day for me.

## B006 · Bộ phim đáng nhớ nhất bạn từng xem? Nội dung và điểm đặc biệt?
- en: What was a memorable movie that you watched in the past? What was it about? What was special about that movie?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

The most memorable movie that I have seen is Titanic.
It is a romantic movie based on the true story of the famous Titanic ship.
My favourite actor also stars in the film.
It tells the sensational love story between Jack and Rose, the two main characters, on a ship that sank on its first journey.
Even though the movie doesn't have a happy ending for the main characters, it brought me to tears many times.
I still remember every scene and every line in the movie until now.
