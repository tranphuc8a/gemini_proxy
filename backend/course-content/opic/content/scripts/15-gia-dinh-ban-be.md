## A02 · Miêu tả 2 người trong gia đình/bạn bè – điểm giống và khác nhau
- en: Tell me about two people in your family or among your friends. How are they similar, and how are they different?
- dang: so-sanh

Sure!
I'd love to talk about two people who are really close to me — my younger brother and my best friend Minh.
My brother is 25, works as a teacher, and honestly he's the kind of person who can light up any room — super outgoing, talkative, always in a good mood.
Minh, on the other hand, is around my age and works in finance.
He's a lot quieter and more reserved — kind of the opposite personality.
But what's interesting is that despite those differences, they actually have a lot in common.
Both of them love being active — they jog and cycle regularly on weekends.
And they're both incredibly dependable.
Whenever I need advice or just someone to talk to, I know I can count on either of them.
I think having both types of people in my life has actually been really good for me — they balance each other out in a nice way.

## A03 · Khi nói chuyện với bạn bè thường nói về vấn đề gì?
- en: What do you usually talk about when you get together with your friends?
- dang: thoi-quen

Hmm, when I hang out or call my friends, we usually end up talking about all sorts of things, to be honest.
Work is probably the most common topic — we swap stories about our jobs, funny office situations, or sometimes ask each other for career advice.
We also talk a lot about movies and shows.
Since I'm pretty into films, I'll often recommend something and then we'll share our thoughts afterward.
Tech comes up a fair amount too, especially since a lot of my close friends work in tech-related areas.
And sometimes — not always, but sometimes — we get into deeper stuff, like life goals, travel plans, or just... venting about a stressful week.
Honestly, I think good conversations with friends are one of the best ways to recharge and get a fresh perspective on things.

## A04 · Chủ đề khi gặp gia đình/bạn bè và ảnh hưởng của việc sống chung
- en: What do you talk about when you meet your family or friends? How has living together with other people influenced you?
- dang: thoi-quen

When I get together with family or friends, the conversations are usually pretty lively and cover a lot of ground.
With family, we catch up on the everyday stuff — how work's going, what we've been eating, any news about relatives.
With friends it tends to be more about shared interests — movies, sports, travel plans, that kind of thing.
In terms of living together — I used to live with my family before I moved to Hanoi for work.
And that really shaped me, I think.
It taught me to be more considerate, to communicate openly, and to share responsibilities.
Now that I live alone, I do sometimes miss that energy.
But living on my own has also given me a real sense of independence and self-discipline, which I genuinely value.

## A05 · Tính cách của hai người trong gia đình/bạn bè: điểm chung và khác biệt
- en: Compare the personalities of two people in your family or among your friends. What do they have in common, and how are they different?
- dang: so-sanh

So, comparing my brother and Minh in terms of personality — they're actually quite different.
My brother is naturally extroverted — enthusiastic, sociable, always looking for the next thing to do or talk about.
Minh is more introverted and thoughtful.
He's the type who listens carefully before he speaks, which I really respect.
One big thing they share, though, is loyalty.
Both of them are genuinely trustworthy — neither would ever let a friend down.
They're also both really hardworking in their own fields, which I find admirable.
The biggest difference is probably how they handle pressure.
My brother stays upbeat and energetic, while Minh gets calm and analytical.
I think both approaches have their strengths.

## A06 · Điều tồi tệ xảy ra với gia đình hoặc bất đồng với gia đình
- en: Tell me about a difficult time or a disagreement in your family. What happened, and how was it resolved?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

One really difficult period for my family was when my grandfather became seriously ill a few years ago.
He had to be hospitalized for several weeks, and the whole family was really worried.
My parents took turns staying at the hospital, which was exhausting — both physically and emotionally.
During that time, there were some disagreements about which hospital to go to and what treatment options to pursue.
Those kinds of tensions are pretty normal under stress, I think.
But in the end, we managed to come to an agreement through open family discussion.
Thankfully, my grandfather did recover after a few months.
And honestly, going through that together brought our family closer.
It really showed us how important communication is when things get hard.

## A07 · Chọn một mâu thuẫn với cha mẹ và kể chi tiết
- en: Tell me about a disagreement you had with your parents. What was it about, and how did it end?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

The biggest disagreement I've had with my parents was about my career path after I graduated.
They really wanted me to find a stable government job close to our hometown — they were worried about job security and, I think, about the distance if I moved to Hanoi.
But I was passionate about working in tech.
I'd already received an offer from Samsung, and I knew it was the right move for me.
We had several long conversations about it — some of them got a bit heated, to be honest.
But I could tell they were coming from a place of genuine concern, not trying to control me.
I tried to explain my reasoning — that the tech industry has strong prospects, and Samsung is a well- respected company.
After a few weeks of back-and-forth, they agreed to support my decision.
And looking back, I'm really grateful for how it turned out — and for how that experience helped us communicate more openly as a family.

## B028 · Mô tả một người thân hoặc bạn bè: người đó thế nào, có gì đặc biệt?
- en: Describe a family or a friend you have What is he or she like? What is special about that person?
- dang: mieu-ta

I'd like to share a little about my parents.
My dad and mom are similar in some ways.
They are very family-oriented, showing great love for each other and their children.
They are willing to do anything to improve and support their family.
However, they also have some differences.
My dad enjoys football, but my mom doesn't.
My mom loves cats, while my dad prefers dogs.
Sometimes, they face conflicts in family life, but they always find a way to resolve them.
That's what my parents are like.
I love them deeply, and I hope to spend more time with them in the future.

## B029 · Bạn thường làm gì khi gặp gỡ bạn bè hoặc gia đình?
- en: Tell me about what you commonly do with your friends or family members when you get together with them?
- dang: thoi-quen

I have some friends who live near my area.
On weekends, we often meet up at a coffee shop or restaurant.
We order drinks and food, and then we start chatting.
We talk about our jobs, our families, and our plans for the future.
Sometimes, we also visit an online game store and play our favorite games together.
They are like family to me—always there to support me when I feel down.
I truly cherish them and love them so much.

## B030 · Kể lần gần đây đến thăm bạn bè / người thân: làm gì, điều gì đáng nhớ?
- en: Talk about a time when you visited a friend or a family member recently. What did you do when you visited them? What was memorable about that visit? Tell me everything from beginning to end.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

Last New Year's Day, I finally returned to my hometown where my parents live.
When I visited them, I brought some gifts and also purchased some new furniture for their home.
We gathered together to celebrate the holiday.
We cooked a variety of traditional foods, especially making Chung cake, which is a traditional cake in my country for New Year's Day.
During the holiday, we enjoyed delicious meals, spent time together, visited neighbors, and attended various festivals.
We left our work behind to fully embrace and enjoy this special occasion.

## B031 · Kể lần gần đây đến thăm bạn (phiên bản: uống say ở nhà bạn).
