## B025 · Giới thiệu công ty bạn: loại hình, thành lập khi nào, ở đâu, sản phẩm gì?
- en: You indicated that you work. I would like to know about your company. What kind of company is it? When was it founded? Where is it located? What products or services does it offer?
- dang: mieu-ta

I work for a large electronics company.
It's a global company that does business worldwide.
I have worked here for about 10 years.
My company is one of the most famous companies in cell phones and home appliances products.
It was founded in the late 1960s.
It was small when it first started, but now it is a huge enterprise.
The headquarters is located in Korea.
There are many factories and R&D centers in several countries, such as Vietnam, China, India, These are some details about the company I work for.

## B026 · Một ngày làm việc điển hình của bạn diễn ra thế nào?
- en: Tell me about your daily routine at work. What kind of work do you engage in on a normal workday? What do you typically do as soon as you arrive at the office? Tell me everything about your day at work?
- dang: thoi-quen

Currently, I am working in the software development department.
My work schedule is quite flexible.
I usually arrive at the office around 8 or 9 AM.
First, I turn on my computer and check all my emails.
Then, I create a task list for everything I need to accomplish that day.
Typically, my working day is quite busy, as I have numerous tasks and projects to handle.
I have regular meetings with my colleagues every morning.
Additionally, I need to respond to and send emails to customers daily.
I often leave the office around 5 or 6 PM, but sometimes I have to work late if there are any urgent or critical issues.
This is my typical working day

## B027 · Kể về một dự án bạn làm tuần trước: loại gì, phải làm gì, họp hành ra sao?
- en: Now, tell me about a project you did at work last week. What kind of project was it? How did it go? What did you have to do for the project? Did you have any meetings to attend for the project?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I had a hard work schedule last week.
It was a new project that we had to finish within one week.
I had a lot of work to do on it.
I had to research new technologies to apply to the project.
I had to create many documents, many reports, join many meetings and update status to my boss.
It seemed like I was burnt out from overworking.
Fortunately, I finished the project on time.
Great feedback from my boss and the customer, and a project bonus for my contributions, made me really happy.

## B112 · Hệ thống giáo dục nước bạn: học bao nhiêu năm, có những cấp nào?
- en: I'd like to know about the educational system in your country. How long do people have to go to school to complete formal education? How many classes are there in schools
- dang: mieu-ta

In Vietnam, students start school at the age of six.
First, they go to primary school for five years, from grade 1 to grade 5.
Then they move to secondary school for four years, from grade 6 to grade 9.
After that, they go to high school for three years, from grade 10 to grade 12.
When they finish high school, they can take the national exam to enter university or college.
So, in total, people study around 12 years to complete formal education in Vietnam.

## B113 · Giáo dục tiếng Anh ở nước bạn: bạn học tiếng Anh thế nào?
- en: Tell me about English education in your country. How did you learn English as a second language? Please tell me about it in as much detail as possible.
- dang: kinh-nghiem

In Vietnam, students start learning English from primary school.
We study grammar, vocabulary, reading and writing in class.
Later, many students go to private English centers to practice speaking and listening.
I started learning English at school, but I improved more by watching English movies and listening to songs.
Now, I try to speak English every day to get better.

## B114 · Giáo dục công lập nước bạn đã thay đổi thế nào? Cách cải thiện quan trọng nhất?
- en: There might have been changes in public school education in your country. What are they? What do you think is the most important way to improve public education in your country?
- dang: y-kien

Yes, there have been many changes in public schools in Vietnam.
Now students use computers and the internet more for studying.
English and soft skills are also more important than before.
To improve public education, I think we should have more good teachers and modern facilities.
That will help students learn better and be more creative.
