## A17 · Thể loại âm nhạc yêu thích? Ca sĩ / nhạc sĩ yêu thích?
- en: You indicated in the survey that you listen to music. What kind of music do you like? Who are your favorite singers or composers?
- dang: mieu-ta

Music is a pretty big part of my day-to-day life.
My favorite genre is probably indie pop and acoustic music — I find it really soothing without being boring.
My favorite Vietnamese artist is Ha Anh Tuan.
He has a warm, emotional voice and his songs are about everyday feelings in a really relatable way.
I also enjoy Ed Sheeran from time to time — his songwriting is very natural and conversational, which I appreciate.
I usually listen on Spotify — during my morning jog, on the commute, or before sleeping.
Music has this incredible ability to shift your mood almost instantly.
After a long day, putting on a calm playlist helps me unwind really quickly.

## A18 · Thể loại nhạc yêu thích? Trước đây và bây giờ có thay đổi không?
- en: What kind of music do you like? Has your taste in music changed compared to the past?
- dang: so-sanh

Definitely, my taste has changed a lot over the years.
Back in middle and high school, I mainly listened to popular Vietnamese pop and K-pop — basically whatever was trending at the time.
I didn't really have a strong personal preference; I just followed what my friends liked.
Then at university, living more independently, I started exploring on YouTube and discovered indie pop, lo-fi beats, and acoustic folk music.
I immediately connected with them — they felt more personal somehow.
Now my everyday playlist is mostly soft indie songs, gentle jazz, and lo-fi music for working or studying.
I think the shift reflects how my lifestyle changed — from a social, school-based life to something more introspective and focused.

## A19 · Âm nhạc hồi bé và hiện tại, tác giả/ca khúc ưa thích
- en: What music did you listen to as a child, and what do you listen to now? Who is your favorite artist, and what is your favorite song?
- dang: so-sanh

When I was young, I didn't really pay much attention to music intentionally — I just heard whatever was on TV or at family gatherings.
I remember my mother humming old Vietnamese folk songs while cooking.
Those melodies still feel really nostalgic to me.
As I got older — especially in high school — I started exploring on my own and discovered artists like Son Tung MTP and some K-pop groups.
Now, my taste has matured quite a bit.
My current favorite is Ha Anh Tuan's album 'Trinh Ca'.
His songs are poetic, emotionally layered, and beautifully arranged with acoustic instruments.
Listening to his music kind of feels like reading a short story — there's always a mood and a narrative being told.
The shift from childhood music to now reflects how my appreciation for depth and craftsmanship has grown.

## A20 · Lần đầu nghe nhạc với ai, ở đâu, phương pháp nghe nhạc
- en: When did you first listen to music? Who were you with, and where? How do you usually listen to music?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

My earliest memory of really listening to music was when I was about 10.
My dad had an old CD player in the living room, and in the evenings he'd play Vietnamese ballads.
I remember sitting with him and just... listening together.
I didn't fully understand the lyrics, but the music felt comforting somehow.
That was also when I first noticed how music could set an atmosphere and create emotion in a room.
As I got older, I moved from MP3 players to smartphones, and now I stream everything on Spotify.
These days, I prefer listening through wireless earphones — usually during my morning jog or when I'm doing focused work.
Good earphones really do make a difference in the listening experience.
And that early memory of listening with my dad is something I still think about fondly.

## A21 · Bắt đầu thích nghe nhạc như thế nào? Sự thay đổi sở thích theo thời gian
- en: How did you first become interested in music? How has your taste in music changed over time?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I became a more conscious music listener during my first year of university.
Before that, music was mostly background noise — I wasn't really stopping to actually listen.
But when I started living in a dormitory away from home, music became a way to cope with homesickness and stress.
I'd put on headphones and just explore different artists for hours.
That's when I discovered acoustic guitar music, and I fell in love with the simplicity and intimacy of it.
Over the years, my preferences shifted from energetic pop to slower, more thoughtful genres.
Now music is a daily thing for me — and it's become an important emotional outlet.
It helps me process feelings that I might not easily put into words.

## B002 · Bạn thường nghe nhạc khi nào, ở đâu? Có nghe radio, đi xem concert không?
- en: When and where do you usually go to listen to music? Do you listen to the radio? Do you go to concerts? Tell me about the different ways you enjoy music.
- dang: thoi-quen

I don't go to concerts.
Most of the time, I listen to music on my cell phone, so I can enjoy it wherever I am.
I usually listen to music in the car when I'm driving, or when I'm walking down the street.
Sometimes, I also listen to music while cooking or cleaning my house.
Even when I'm bored, I listen to music because it helps improve my mood and reduce stress very effectively.

## B003 · Bạn bắt đầu thích nhạc từ khi nào? Sở thích thay đổi ra sao từ bé đến giờ?
- en: When did you first become interested in music? What kinds of music did you like at first? Tell me how your interest in music developed from your childhood until today?
- dang: so-sanh

I don't remember exactly, but I think I have loved music since I was a kid.
At that time we were poor, so I didn't have the opportunity to listen to many kinds of music.
I just listened to traditional music on my family's old radio and the songs that my mom and my grandmother sang.
Now, life has become better, and I can access and listen to many kinds of music.
Sometimes I listen to pop music, sometimes rock or country music, and sometimes EDM when I exercise.
In conclusion, I listen to many types of music now.

## B003b · Kể một kỷ niệm đáng nhớ liên quan đến âm nhạc.
- en: Can you tell me about a memorable experience with music?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

Yes, I remember one special time when I went to a live concert of my favorite singer.
It was about three years ago.
The concert was held in a big stadium, and there were so many fans.
When the singer came on stage, everyone was screaming and singing along.
I felt so excited and happy because it was the first time I saw my idol in real life.
That night was unforgettable, and it made me love music even more.
