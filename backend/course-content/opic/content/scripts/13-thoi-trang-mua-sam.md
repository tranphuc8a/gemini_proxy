## B052 · Các cửa hàng / trung tâm mua sắm ở nước bạn trông thế nào?
- en: You indicated in the survey that you like to go shopping. Talk about stores or shopping centers in your country. What are they like? Describe them in detail.
- dang: mieu-ta

There are tons of stores in Vietnam.
They are everywhere these days, especially on crowded streets with heavy traffic.
One of the largest places to shop is outlet malls.
There are many types of stores in outlet malls.
You can buy food, clothes, furniture, electronic devices, and so on.
You can often get discounts or bargain for the price of the product you want to buy.
So, whether the store is small or large, there are plenty of stores in my country.

## B053 · Bạn đi mua sắm ở đâu, khi nào, mua gì? Nơi đó có gì đặc biệt?
- en: Where do you go and when do you go shopping for something? What do you buy when you go there? What is special about that place?
- dang: thoi-quen

I go to various places to shop, such as outlet malls or department stores.
However, I prefer to shop online.
Nowadays, online shopping is very convenient in many ways.
It saves me time and energy when I buy the things I need.
When I want to buy food or clothes, I usually go to physical stores because I believe they should be checked directly before buying.
But for things like electronic tools, equipment, or books, I usually buy them online.
So, I go shopping whenever I need something, but overall, I prefer online shopping.

## B054 · Ký ức mua sắm hồi nhỏ: cửa hàng nào, trông thế nào, ấn tượng gì?
- en: Think of your early memories of shopping. Was there a store you remember from you childhood? What did it look like and what were your impressions of that place?
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember there was a grocery store near my house when I was a kid.
Even though it was small, it had everything I needed.
The prices were also very cheap, and sometimes we could bargain to get a better deal.
I used to go there to buy candy, ice cream, or milk.
Sometimes I also bought toys or school supplies.
The shop owner was very friendly with customers.
That is one of my earliest memories of shopping.

## B055 · Người nước bạn thường mặc gì? Đi làm và đi chơi có khác nhau không?
- en: What kinds of clothes do people in your country typically wear? Do they wear different clothes for work or for play?
- dang: mieu-ta

Life is very modern now.
Fashion and style are everywhere and influence everyone.
Vietnamese people are the same — they are very stylish.
They like to follow the latest fashion trends and wear different clothes for each season.
Fashion and styles also change depending on the season.
Plus, people wear different types of clothes depending on where they are — at the office, going out with friends, or attending an event.
So, I can say that people in my country are very fashionable these days.

## B056 · Bạn thích mặc kiểu gì? Hôm nay bạn mặc gì?
- en: What kind of clothes do you like to wear personally? What kind of fashion style do you like? What are you wearing today?
- dang: mieu-ta

Today, I am wearing a black T-shirt and blue jeans.
This is also my usual style.
I like a simple and strong look.
I usually wear a T-shirt and jeans with sneakers.
Most of my clothes are white, blue, or black — very simple colors.
So, that's my style: simple and strong.

## B057 · Thời trang hồi bạn còn nhỏ khác gì bây giờ?
- en: Fashion styles are always changing. Tell me about the kinds of clothes that were popular when you were younger. How are they different from what is popular now?
- dang: so-sanh

Actually, when I was a kid, I didn't have enough clothes to wear.
I had to use old clothes from my brother and cousins.
Most of my friends were the same as me at that time.
So, fashion style when I was young was not very stylish.
But now, the quality of life has become much better.
There are many styles and fashion trends, and people always update themselves with the latest trends, following famous models.
I see people wearing beautiful and fashionable clothes everywhere.
So, fashion between the past and now is very different.
