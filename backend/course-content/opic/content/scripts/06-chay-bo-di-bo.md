## A57 · Chạy bộ với ai, làm gì trước/sau khi chạy, tại sao thích chạy bộ?
- en: You indicated in the survey that you jog. Who do you jog with? What do you do before and after jogging? Why do you like jogging?
- dang: thoi-quen

I usually jog by myself, which I actually prefer — it gives me quiet time to think and decompress.
Before going, I always drink a glass of water and do about 5 minutes of dynamic stretching to warm up.
I run for 30 to 40 minutes, usually around the lake in the park near my apartment.
After finishing, I walk slowly to cool down, then go home to shower and have breakfast.
I started jogging about two years ago when I realized I was sitting at a desk for 8+ hours a day and not getting nearly enough physical activity.
What I love most is the mental clarity it brings.
After a good run, my mind feels calm and organized — which is really invaluable before a full workday.
It's become a habit that helps me feel structured and disciplined overall.

## B102 · Chạy bộ khác yoga thế nào? Môn nào tốt hơn cho bạn?
- en: Please tell me about the difference between jogging and another kind of sports. For example let's say the the other sport is Yoga. How are they different from each other? Which one do you think is better for exercise?
- dang: so-sanh

Jogging and yoga are very different from each other.
Jogging is a kind of outdoor exercise.
It helps me build strong legs and burn calories.
I usually sweat a lot when I jog.
On the other hand, yoga focuses on stretching and relaxing the body.
It helps with balance, flexibility, and peace of mind.
If my goal is to lose weight, jogging is more effective.
But if I am looking to relax and reduce stress, yoga is a better choice.
Personally, I prefer jogging more because it gives me more energy for the day.

## B103 · Kể một kỷ niệm đáng nhớ khi chạy bộ: chuyện gì xảy ra, vì sao đáng nhớ?
- en: Tell me about one of the most memorable episodes you have had jogging. What happened? Why is that so memorable for you? Tell me the whole story of that episode
- dang: kinh-nghiem

I remember one time when I went jogging in the park near my house last year.
That morning, the weather was very nice and cool, so I decided to jog a few laps.
When I was jogging, I suddenly saw a little dog running after me.
At first, I was scared, but soon I realized it just wanted to play.
The dog followed me for almost one kilometer, and it made me laugh a lot.
Later, its owner came and thanked me for keeping the dog safe.
It was a small thing, but it made my day special.
That's why this jogging episode is so memorable for me.
